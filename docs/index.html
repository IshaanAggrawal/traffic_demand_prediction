Project Objective

The goal of this project is to accurately predict traffic demand at specific geographic locations (geohashes) and 15-minute time slots. The system learns historical patterns to forecast future demand, which is crucial for urban planning, traffic management, and dynamic resource allocation.

The Challenge

Spatial Disjoint: Zero overlap in (geohash, slot) combinations between training Day 49 and testing Day 49 sets.


Solution: The problem translates to learning generalizable temporal patterns from Day 48 to reliably forecast for unobserved locations on Day 49, completely mitigating data leakage.

Architecture & Pipeline

1. Robust Validation

To mirror the real-world test distribution and prevent data leakage, models trained on Day 48 are validated on Day 49. Early stopping iterations are discovered here before full retraining.

2. Advanced Feature Engineering

Implemented hierarchical target encoding with spatial fallbacks (geo4, geo5), cyclical time encodings via sine/cosine for temporal continuity, and complex interaction features.

3. Optimal Blending Ensemble

Combines LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost. Final predictions are a weighted ensemble where optimal weights are derived mathematically using the SciPy minimize function to maximize the validation R² score.
